Gotham Villain

The Joker

The Joker is Batman’s most dangerous enemy, a chaotic criminal mastermind who uses madness, fear, and violence to destroy order.

About the Joker

The Joker is one of the most iconic villains in Gotham. He thrives on chaos and believes that life is meaningless unless it is wrapped in madness and destruction. His battles with Batman are not only physical but psychological, testing the hero’s will and morality.
