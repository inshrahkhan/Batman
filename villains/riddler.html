The Riddler

The Riddler is a brilliant criminal who uses puzzles, riddles, and mind games to challenge Batman and expose Gotham’s hidden truths.

About the Riddler

Edward Nygma, known as the Riddler, is a master of logic and deception. He does not merely commit crimes; he creates elaborate tests that force Batman to think like a detective in the truest sense.
