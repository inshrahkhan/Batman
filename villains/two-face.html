Gotham Villain

Two-Face

Two-Face was once Gotham’s idealistic district attorney, but a tragic accident fractured his life and turned him into a violent criminal.

About Two-Face

Harvey Dent’s fall into madness made him a symbol of how trauma and corruption can destroy a once-promising life. His duality is reflected in the two sides of his personality and his coin.
