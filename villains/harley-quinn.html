Gotham Villain

Harley Quinn

Harley Quinn began as a psychiatrist at Arkham Asylum and became one of Gotham’s most unpredictable villains after falling under the Joker’s influence.

About Harley Quinn

Harley Quinn is known for her wild energy, sharp wit, and dangerous unpredictability. She is both a villain and a tragic figure, shaped by obsession, trauma, and a constant struggle to define herself beyond the Joker.
