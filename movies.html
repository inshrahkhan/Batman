Batman Movies in Release Order

A complete guide to the major Batman films, from the early classics to the modern Dark Knight era.

Batman (1966)

Summary: A campy, colorful adaptation of the Caped Crusader that follows Batman and Robin as they battle Gotham’s villains with gadgets and detective work.

Cast: Adam West, Burt Ward, Lee Meriwether, Cesar Romero

Batman (1989)

Summary: Tim Burton’s dark reimagining of Batman begins with Bruce Wayne becoming Gotham’s vigilante and facing the criminal mastermind Jack Napier, who becomes the Joker.

Cast: Michael Keaton, Jack Nicholson, Kim Basinger, Robert Wuhl

Batman Returns (1992)

Summary: Batman faces the Penguin and Catwoman while trying to protect Gotham from a wave of corruption and chaos.

Cast: Michael Keaton, Danny DeVito, Michelle Pfeiffer, Christopher Walken

Batman Forever (1995)

Summary: Batman confronts Two-Face and the Riddler as Gotham descends into a darker, more theatrical crisis.

Cast: Val Kilmer, Tommy Lee Jones, Jim Carrey, Nicole Kidman

Batman & Robin (1997)

Summary: Batman and Robin team up with Batgirl to battle Mr. Freeze, Poison Ivy, and Bane in a glossy, high-energy adventure.

Cast: George Clooney, Chris O’Donnell, Alicia Silverstone, Arnold Schwarzenegger

Batman Begins (2005)

Summary: Bruce Wayne trains in the shadows and returns to Gotham as Batman to stop corruption and fear from consuming the city.

Cast: Christian Bale, Michael Caine, Liam Neeson, Katie Holmes, Gary Oldman

The Dark Knight (2008)

Summary: Batman faces the Joker, a criminal mastermind who pushes Gotham to its limits and tests the hero’s moral boundaries.

Cast: Christian Bale, Heath Ledger, Aaron Eckhart, Maggie Gyllenhaal, Gary Oldman

The Dark Knight Rises (2012)

Summary: After years of peace, Batman returns to stop Bane from destroying Gotham and its people.

Cast: Christian Bale, Tom Hardy, Anne Hathaway, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Gary Oldman

Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice (2016)

Summary: Batman and Superman clash over ideology and power while a larger threat emerges in the form of Lex Luthor and Doomsday.

Cast: Ben Affleck, Henry Cavill, Gal Gadot, Jesse Eisenberg

Suicide Squad (2016)

Summary: Although not a solo Batman film, the movie features Batman as a major presence in Gotham’s criminal underworld and the wider DC universe.

Cast: Will Smith, Jared Leto, Margot Robbie, Viola Davis

Justice League (2017)

Summary: Batman joins other heroes to stop Steppenwolf and protect Earth from destruction, showing his leadership beyond Gotham.

Cast: Ben Affleck, Gal Gadot, Ezra Miller, Jason Momoa

The Batman (2022)

Summary: A gritty detective story in which Batman investigates a string of murders connected to Gotham’s corruption and the Riddler.

Cast: Robert Pattinson, Zoë Kravitz, Paul Dano, Colin Farrell, Jeffrey Wright

The Flash (2023)

Summary: Batman appears in a multiverse adventure that brings together different versions of heroes and villains, including a version of Bruce Wayne.

Cast: Ezra Miller, Michael Keaton, Ben Affleck, Sasha Calle
